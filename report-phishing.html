Before you do anything else

  • 01Don't click any links or open attachments in the email.
  • 02Don't reply to the sender, even to ask questions.
  • 03If it claims to be from someone you know, verify through a different channel — call them, don't use contact details from the email.
  • 04If you already clicked a link or entered credentials, change that password immediately and notify your IT or security team.
  • 05Forward the full email, including headers if possible, using the form here or to your internal security team.
