Accuracy & methodology

Verified, not just scraped.

A search engine gives you a list of links. An investigation gives you a conclusion you can act on. Every finding is cross-referenced against at least one independent source before it's included, and rated by how confident we are in it — so you know the difference between a confirmed fact and a lead worth watching.

  • → Findings cross-checked against multiple independent sources
  • → Reviewed by a human analyst — not delivered as a raw data export
  • → Confidence-rated, so speculation is never presented as fact
  • → Full source trail retained for every report, in case it's ever questioned
